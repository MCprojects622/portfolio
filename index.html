About Me

Builder by nature,
thinker by instinct

"I don't want a job that I tolerate. I want work that I love."

I spent years doing work that didn't fit — tolerating jobs at great companies, feeling the gap between what I was doing and what I was capable of. Then I started building.

What I discovered is that I've been a product thinker my entire life. Every time I saw a problem — a fragmented NYC rental market, a need for personal business accountability — I didn't just notice it. I built something to solve it.

Now I'm channeling that into a career that fits who I actually am: someone who obsesses over how things work, how they could work better, and how to make them real.

And it turns out building has always been my language — not just apps and databases, but a published guidebook that helped people design their dream lives, blogs I ran for years, and words I discovered I could use to move people. The through line was always the same: see a gap, think of a fix, make it real.

NYC Rental Intelligence Database

A personal MLS where none existed

As a NYC rental agent, I was losing deals because there was no central system to track luxury buildings, availability, unit types, and guarantor policies across a fragmented market. So I built one. A searchable database of 4,000+ data points across three datasets — guarantor-accepted buildings, luxury inventory, and open rentals — organized to eliminate blind outreach and match clients to properties faster.

Clarifi

Your financial clarity, not a budget app

Most finance apps lead with restriction and guilt. Clarifi leads with awareness. Built around one question — where do I stand right now? — it gives you a real-time snapshot of your cards, income streams, upcoming payments, bills, subscriptions, and debt. No budget. No judgment. Just clarity. Designed, built, branded, and shipped with a full login system, cloud sync, and a Berry Bright visual identity that makes money feel approachable instead of anxiety-inducing.
