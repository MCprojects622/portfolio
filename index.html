About Me

The creativity came first.
The building followed.

"I don't want a life I tolerate. I want a life I designed."

Before there were apps, there were words. Blogs I ran because I had something to say. A wellness brand I built because I wanted to inspire people. A guidebook I wrote and published because I believed in the power of helping someone design their own life. Creation has always been my first language.

The building came after. When I saw problems that needed solving, I stopped waiting for someone else to solve them. A fragmented NYC rental market. Entrepreneurs with no way to track their progress. People who couldn't see their finances clearly. I built something for each one.

Now both live together. Every product I build is rooted in creativity and led by empathy. Every piece of content I create is built with intention. I am a human first -- which means I see every user as a person with real issues in need of real solutions, not just a consumer to build for. That is the lens through which I see everything.

Some of what I've built has failed. I count those too. Failure is just data. And the through line across all of it has always been the same: see a gap, think and design a fix, make it real.

NYC Rental Intelligence Database

I needed it. It didn't exist. So I built it.

As a NYC rental agent I was losing deals because the market was fragmented and there was no central system to track it. So I built one — a searchable database of 4,000+ data points across three datasets covering guarantor policies, luxury inventory, and open rentals. It eliminated blind outreach, sped up client matching, and gave me an operational edge. A business tool built out of necessity — the purest kind.
